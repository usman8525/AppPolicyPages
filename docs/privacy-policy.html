Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026 Package: com.logicx.boardgame

Quick Summary

  • BoardBox does not require an account, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Player names, onboarding status, and sound preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • The app uses Google AdMob to show ads and Google User Messaging Platform to request consent where required.
  • The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to help us detect crashes and improve stability.

1. About This App

BoardBox is a free board games app developed by LogicX Universe. It includes Tic Tac Toe, Connect Four, and Dots and Boxes. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled by the app, why it is used, and what third-party services are involved.

By installing or using BoardBox, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

BoardBox stores a small amount of app data locally on your device using device storage. This local data is used only to make the app work smoothly.

  • Player name you enter in onboarding or profile settings.
  • Whether onboarding has been completed.
  • Your sound on/off preference.
  • Game state and scores during active gameplay sessions.
Locally stored gameplay/profile settings are not uploaded to our own servers. Uninstalling the app removes this local app data from your device.

3. Advertising and Consent

BoardBox uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. The app is currently configured to request non-personalized ads. Google and its advertising partners may still process information such as device identifiers, advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes such as ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention, and compliance.

Where required by law or Google policy, BoardBox uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent for ads and related data processing. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be non-personalized, limited, or not shown.

You can also manage advertising choices through your Android device settings, including resetting or deleting your advertising ID where supported by your device.

Important: We do not directly receive your Google advertising profile or a list of ads you personally see. Ad serving and consent handling are managed by Google AdMob and Google's SDKs.

4. Crash Reporting

BoardBox uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes, understand stability problems, and improve the app. Crash reports may include crash stack traces, app version, operating system version, device model, device state at the time of the crash, timestamps, and installation identifiers such as a Firebase installation ID or Crashlytics installation UUID.

Crashlytics reports are used for diagnostics and app improvement. They are processed by Google Firebase according to Google's Firebase terms and privacy practices.

5. App Permissions — What We Use and Why

The exact permissions in an Android build may include permissions required by the app itself and permissions automatically added by Google SDKs used for ads, crash reporting, and platform services. The main permissions are explained below.

Permission / Access Why it is used Data transmitted?
INTERNET Required for Google AdMob ads, consent messages, Firebase Crashlytics reports, and Google Play services. To Google SDKs
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used by Google SDKs to understand network availability before loading ads or sending crash reports. Limited
AD_ID / Ad Services permissions Used by Google advertising services for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and privacy-preserving ad features on supported Android versions. To Google SDKs

6. Third-Party Services

BoardBox uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — used to show ads and measure ad performance.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — used to request and manage ad consent where required.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — used to collect crash diagnostics and improve app stability.
  • Google Play Services — used for Android platform services such as ads, app review, and related Google SDK functionality.

These services are provided by Google and are governed by Google's privacy practices. You can learn more from the following resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob privacy and UMP information
  • Firebase privacy and security information

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate our own backend database for player profiles or gameplay history.

Data may be processed by Google services integrated into the app, including AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics, as described above. We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable government request.

8. Children's Privacy

BoardBox is intended for users aged 13 and over. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and it does not require account creation.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the concern.

9. Data Retention

Local app preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Session gameplay scores are temporary and may be cleared when the app closes or the game resets.

Firebase Crashlytics data is retained by Google Firebase according to Firebase's retention practices. Google's Firebase privacy documentation states that Crashlytics crash stack traces, minidump data, and associated identifiers are retained for a limited period before deletion from live and backup systems.

10. Data Security

We use reputable third-party services provided by Google for ads, consent, and crash reporting. Local app data is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device security settings. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, especially when app features or third-party services change. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
