Tic Tac Toe, Connect 4, Boxes

Privacy Policy

1. About This App

Tic Tac Toe, Connect 4, Boxes is a free, offline-first board games app developed by LogicX Universe (package: com.logicx.boardgame). It includes three classic games: Tic Tac Toe, Connect Four, and Dots and Boxes. This Privacy Policy describes what data the app accesses, why, and how it is handled.

By installing or using Tic Tac Toe, Connect 4, Boxes, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Collect

Tic Tac Toe, Connect 4, Boxes does not collect any personal data. We do not operate any servers, databases, or backend services that receive data from your device.

The only data the app handles is stored entirely on your device:

  • Player names you optionally type before a match (max 12 characters, stored in device-local AsyncStorage)
  • Round scores within a session (kept in memory only, cleared when the app closes)
This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, shared, or accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data.

3. App Permissions — What We Use and Why

Tic Tac Toe, Connect 4, Boxes requests exactly two Android permissions. The table below explains the reason for each one and whether any data is transmitted as a result.

Permission Why it is used Data transmitted?
INTERNET Required by the Google Play In-App Review API to display a native review prompt after gameplay. No data is sent to us — this is handled entirely by Google's system. None to us
VIBRATE Powers haptic feedback (tactile vibration) on moves, wins, and menu interactions via expo-haptics. Operates entirely on-device. None
Note on INTERNET permission: Although the app requests INTERNET access, all three games function 100% offline. Internet is used solely by the Google Play In-App Review API to show a native review dialog. This is a Google-managed process — we do not receive, log, or store any information from this interaction.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third parties — because we do not collect any user data in the first place.

The only external system the app communicates with is the Google Play In-App Review API, which is governed by Google's own privacy terms. We have no visibility into or control over what Google collects through that API.

5. Children's Privacy

Tic Tac Toe, Connect 4, Boxes is rated for users aged 13 and over on Google Play. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because the app collects no personal data whatsoever and requires no account creation, it is safe for all ages in practice. If you are a parent or guardian with concerns, please contact us at the address below.

6. Data Security

Since Tic Tac Toe, Connect 4, Boxes does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data at risk of a breach on our end. The small amount of local data stored on your device (optional player names) is protected by your device's own security mechanisms, including screen lock and Android's app sandboxing.
